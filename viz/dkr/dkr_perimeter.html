Sensor

Elevation FOV above 15° means the 8-element columns are split into digitised sub-columns (30° = 32 ch, 45° = 48 ch, 60° = 64 ch) and beamformed in elevation: every sector is a full-gain beam, all at once, 20 Hz, no servo. Range is preserved only if the TX PA is raised to keep EIRP at the §15.245 cap (the BOM sits 6.7 dB under it).

Two beams per node. Fan: 90° × 15°, Mini 3 body ~600 m (BOM 500 m at 0.01 m²), cars to the zone edge. Overhead cone: 120° × 120° straight up, ~130 m. The BOM fixes the fan at +10°; set "Elevation sectors" above 1 to nod it instead. BOM assumes 4 nodes for 360°. Price at 110% margin: $4,470/node (prototype cost $2,129) → $1,440/node at volume ($686). 8 nodes: $35.8k proto / $11.5k volume.

Published: AR-300 X-band, 120° × 120°, >300 m on a Mavic (~270 m for a Mini 3), 7 Hz, air only. SR-500 5.8 GHz, 120° × 30°, vehicle 600 m, person 400 m, drone ~100 m. Magos recommends four AR-300 for 360°. No list price. Estimate: SR-500 ~$6k new (a used SR-500F resells at $4,000), AR-300 ~$25k (drone-radar tier; Echodyne panels are ~$40k) → ~$31k per AR-300 + SR-500 pair, range $23–43k. 8 pairs: ~$248k, hardware only.
